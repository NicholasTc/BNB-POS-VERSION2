# Placeholder product artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for the user-approved cashier redesign (2026-10-18). Temporary illustrations of products; existing menu names/prices were not replaced. Optimized WebP copies are bundled locally so default thumbnails need no external service. Owner menu management can select these paths or an HTTPS image URL; blank uses a category fallback. Failed remote images fall back locally, with no referrer sent. The green LISI mark is an editable SVG in `src/components/pos-icons.tsx` based on the reference, not an original brand asset.

Final paths:
- `public/menu/mango.webp`
- `public/menu/strawberry.webp`
- `public/menu/coffee.webp`
- `public/menu/pastry.webp`
- `public/menu/avocado.webp`
- `public/menu/tea.webp`
- `public/menu/water.webp`

Prompt set: photorealistic portrait product photography for an Indonesian café POS thumbnail, light natural stone counter, soft daylight, softly blurred green foliage, warm ivory/forest green palette, whole product filling the central 75%, no text, branding or watermark. Subjects: (1) tall glass of golden mango juice with mango cubes, mint and mango slices; (2) tall strawberry smoothie with strawberries and mint; (3) iced milk coffee with espresso swirls, ice and coffee beans; (4) flaky golden butter croissant on a white ceramic plate; (5) pale green avocado smoothie with mint and an avocado half; (6) amber iced tea with ice and a lemon wedge; (7) unbranded mineral water bottle with a green cap. Images were generated separately, then resized/encoded as WebP for the project.
